I participated in the [Erdős Institute](https://www.erdosinstitute.org/)'s Data Science Boot Camp. In addition to attending video lectures and completing exercises on the theory and practice of data science, I worked on a group project on developmental linguistics and language acquisition. We used developmental data from Stanford's [Wordbank](https://langcog.github.io/wordbank-datapage/) dataset to engineer new features, added them to a [dataset derived from the results of Duolingo users](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/8SWHNO), and measured how much they improved predictions of second-language learning.

You can find our article, slides, and a video of our presentation [here](https://www.erdosinstitute.org/certificates/fall-2025/data-science-boot-camp/sam-schiavone/5f895377-a0ab-42a9-bfdd-11e453beee5c), and our code [here](https://github.com/Erdos-Projects/fall-2025-developmental-norms-and-language-acquisition).
