Day 1: Geometry of solutions to linear systems

Below is a geometrical representation of the following linear system.

$$ \left\{ \begin{array}{ccccc} -2 x_1 & +2 x_2 & +10 x_3 & = & 2\\ -3 x_1 & + x_2 & + 9 x_3 & = & 5\\ 4 x_1 & -2 x_2 & - 14 x_3 & = & -6 \end{array}\right. $$

Using row reduction, we found a simpler equivalent system.

$$ \left\{ \begin{array}{ccccc} x_1 & & - 2x_3 & = & -2\\ & x_2 & + 3 x_3 & = & -1\\ \end{array} \right. $$
